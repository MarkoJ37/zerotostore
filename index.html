FAQ

Honest answers to fair questions

Who is this course for?

Complete beginners and early-stage store owners. If you've never touched Shopify, Module 1 walks you through everything. If you have a store that isn't selling, Modules 2, 4 and 5 are where you'll find the fixes.

How much time do I need per week?

Plan for 4–6 hours a week. The course is self-paced — most students launch their store within 4–6 weeks, but you can go faster or slower. Lifetime access means no pressure.

Does Shopify cost extra?

Yes — Shopify is a separate subscription (they offer a cheap trial period for new stores, so you can build most of your store before paying full price). We show you how to keep total startup costs minimal.

Do I need to pay for AI tools?

No. Every AI workflow in the course works with free tiers of the tools we use. Paid plans speed some things up, but they're optional and we clearly mark them as such.

Do I need money for ads to make this work?

Not to start. Module 4 begins with free, organic traffic methods and only introduces paid ads once your store has proven it can convert. When you do run ads, we teach you how to test with small budgets.

What's the refund policy?

14-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. Go through the first modules, and if it's not what you expected, email us for a full refund.
